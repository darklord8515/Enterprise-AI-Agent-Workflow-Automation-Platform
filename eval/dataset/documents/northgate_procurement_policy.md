# Northgate Group Supplier Procurement Policy

This is a fictional sample policy created for testing. Northgate Group is not a real organisation.

## Scope

This policy applies to all suppliers that provide manufactured components or handle Northgate data.
Procurement staff must collect evidence for every requirement before a supplier is approved.

## Supplier requirements

- REQ-001: The supplier must hold a current ISO 9001 quality management certification.
- REQ-002: The supplier must hold ISO/IEC 27001 information security certification or a SOC 2 Type II report.
- REQ-003: The supplier must maintain cyber liability insurance cover of at least £5 million.
- REQ-004: The supplier must have a business continuity plan that has been tested within the last 12 months.
- REQ-005: Personal data must be processed and stored only within the UK or the European Economic Area.
- REQ-006: The supplier must publish a modern slavery statement.
- REQ-007: The supplier must accept payment terms of 60 days from invoice.
- REQ-008: The supplier must provide audited financial statements for the last two financial years.

## Review

Findings must be reviewed by a procurement manager. Missing evidence must be requested from the supplier.
