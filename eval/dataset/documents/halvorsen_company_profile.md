# Halvorsen Precision Parts Ltd: Company Profile

Fictional sample supplier document created for testing.

## Overview

Halvorsen Precision Parts Ltd manufactures machined aluminium components and employs 180 people at its Sheffield site.
Revenue for the financial year ending March 2026 was £24 million.

## Data protection

All customer personal data is processed and stored in data centres located in the United Kingdom and Ireland.

## Ethical sourcing

Halvorsen publishes an annual modern slavery statement on its website, most recently approved by the board in June 2026.

## Commercial terms

Halvorsen accepts payment terms of 60 days from the date of invoice for framework customers.
